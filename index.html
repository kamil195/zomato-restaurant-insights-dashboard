Business objective

Turn restaurant data into clear decisions

The project focuses on location coverage, cuisine popularity, customer sentiment, and service availability so a stakeholder can quickly compare restaurant-market patterns.

Questions explored

Where are restaurants concentrated? Which cuisines dominate? How do ratings vary? How common are table booking and online delivery? Which cities and brands stand out?

Tools demonstrated

Power BI Desktop, Power Query, data modeling, DAX, KPI cards, slicers, bar charts, combo charts, donut charts, report filtering, and dashboard composition.

Selected findings

What the analysis surfaced

These figures come from the current project documentation in this repository.

India dominates coverage

8,652 restaurants in the dataset are located in India.

New Delhi leads by city

New Delhi has 5,473 restaurants, the largest city-level presence.

North Indian leads cuisine count

North Indian appears in 3,960 restaurant records.

Ratings cluster in the 3–4 band

The largest rating group contains roughly 4.6K restaurants.

Table booking is uncommon

About 1.16K support table booking versus roughly 8.39K that do not.

Online delivery remains limited

About 2.45K offer online delivery versus roughly 7.1K that do not.
